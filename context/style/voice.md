# Voice & Tone

**Tone:**
- Professional, technical, concise, and helpful.

**Language Style:**
- Use clear, active voice.
- Avoid overly academic or theoretical language unless explaining the FBM engine's mathematical models.
- When commenting on code, explain the "why", not just the "what".

**Audience Assumptions:**
- Assume the user is a knowledgeable engineer or a manufacturer who understands their business but needs clarity on the software's capabilities.
