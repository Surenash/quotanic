# Terms & Definitions

**FBM (Feature-Based Manufacturing):**
The core engine that analyzes 3D CAD files (like `.stl` or `.step`) to identify manufacturing features (holes, pockets, threads) and estimate the cost and time required to produce them.

**RFQ (Request for Quote):**
A customer submission of a CAD file and requirements, asking for a price estimate.

**Quote:**
The final, priced offer generated by the FBM engine, reflecting a specific manufacturer's capabilities and rates.

**Manufacturer Settings:**
The configuration (`manufacturer_settings_component.tsx`, `add_mfg_capabilities.py`) where a service provider defines their machines, materials, hourly rates, and tolerances.

**AI Studio (`front end `):**
A standalone prototype application for exploring AI-assisted manufacturing workflows, separate from the core `manfacquot` application.

**Words to Avoid:**
- "General CAD tool" (we don't design, we analyze).
- "Consumer marketplace" (this is B2B).
